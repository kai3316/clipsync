First time

Three steps to get started

  • Install ClipSync on both devices. Windows, macOS and Linux all work, and they can talk to each other.
  • Join the same Wi-Fi and connect once. Both show the same 8-digit number; check it and confirm. They reconnect on their own afterwards.
  • Copy and paste as usual. Copy on one computer and paste on the other; drop files on the window to send them.

Privacy

Your content stays close to your devices

On the same Wi-Fi, the computers find each other directly. On different networks, content is encrypted before it passes through a relay. Neither way needs a cloud account holding your clipboard.

  • Check the numbers when pairing. Both devices show the same 8-digit number; confirm it matches before trusting each other. A later change to the other device's identity raises an alert.
  • Encrypted when it leaves the device. Across networks, content is encrypted before it is sent; the relay only forwards it.
  • Sensitive content can be covered. Card numbers, national ID numbers, phone numbers, passwords and more can become [FILTERED]; choose the categories in Settings.
  • Your history, your rules. How long it is kept, when it is cleared and whether it is saved with a password are all under your control.

Phone

Scan once, carry on from your phone

The computer shows a QR code. Scan it with the phone's camera or browser and the same clipboard history opens. Add it to the home screen on iOS or Android and it is one tap away.

  • Read the computer's clipboard history, copy something to the phone, or send an item back
  • Upload files from the phone and download files from the computer
  • Chat with the computer and follow file transfers
  • Change or close the access code from the computer whenever you want

FAQ

You may also be wondering

Do both computers need to be on the same Wi-Fi?

No. On the same Wi-Fi it is easiest and they find each other automatically; on different networks, turn on internet sync and keep going.

Do I need an account?

No. Install, pair the devices and you are ready; there is no sign-up or login.

Does my clipboard go to the cloud?

On the same Wi-Fi, content travels directly between your devices. Across networks it is encrypted before a relay forwards it; the relay does not keep it.

Do I need an app on my phone?

No. Show the QR code on the computer and open it in the phone's browser. You can add it to the home screen for one-tap access.

Can Windows and Mac sync with each other?

Yes. Windows, macOS and Linux computers can pair, sync the clipboard and send files to one another.

Does ClipSync keep running when I close the window?

Yes. Closing the window puts it in the tray and clipboard sync continues. Use Quit in the tray menu to exit for real.

Do I need to install anything else first?

No. Download the installer for your system, install it and open the app.

What happens when the history gets large?

You can set a retention limit, check how much space it uses and clear all history in Settings. Saved favourites are not cleared.

macOS says the app cannot be verified. What now?

Right-click ClipSync in Finder, choose Open and confirm. If it still says the app is damaged, run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/ClipSync.app in Terminal and open it again.

Is ClipSync free?

It is free for personal and other noncommercial use. Commercial use in a company, paid product or hosted service needs a commercial license first.
